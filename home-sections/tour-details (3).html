Trip Notes & Important Info

Meeting Point & Time

Your Egyptologist guide will meet you at your Cairo or Giza hotel lobby at 8:00 AM. Please be ready 10 minutes before departure.

Dress Code

Comfortable, breathable clothing recommended. Shoulders and knees should be covered when entering mosques. Sunscreen and a hat are strongly advised.

Photography

Photography is permitted at most sites. Interior camera permits may be required at an additional local fee. No photography in certain temple chambers.

Accessibility

This tour involves moderate walking on uneven terrain. Wheelchair access is limited at some sites. Please inform us of any mobility requirements in advance.

Meals & Dietary Requirements

Lunch is included at a quality restaurant. Vegetarian and vegan options are available. Please notify us of any food allergies at the time of booking.

Health & Safety

Bring personal medications and a small first-aid kit. Stay hydrated throughout the day. Our guides carry a first-aid kit and emergency contacts at all times.

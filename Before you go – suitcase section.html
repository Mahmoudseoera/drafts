Before you go

Pack these eight things and you’re ready for sun, stone and desert nights.

Walking shoes

Broken-in, closed-toe soles for stone and sand.

Light scarf

Cover shoulders at mosques and shade your neck.

Plug adapter

Sockets are type C and F at 220 V.

Warm layer

Desert evenings get cold, especially Nov–Feb.

Sunscreen SPF 50+

Reapply every two hours. The sun is strong all year.

Wide-brim hat

Temples and tombs have little shade.

Sunglasses

UV lenses cut the glare off sand and stone.

Water bottle

Carry a litre or more on sightseeing days.
